## Findings

### F-001 — capability re-check text hides executable probe evidence

- Impact: Trust-Damage.
- Affected rows: 5 and 15.
- Actor and entry point: maintainer running
  `roundfix baseline capabilities check --format text` after an executable
  capability candidate is rejected.
- Expected: the human divergence names the inspected PATH candidate or its
  absence and the rejection reason, such as `broken-link`, matching the
  evaluated probe evidence and the documented contract.
- Actual: JSON includes the candidate `sourcePath` and `detail: broken-link`,
  while text only reports that local evidence is insufficient and recommends
  installing the tool. The maintainer cannot tell which candidate failed or
  why without changing to the machine format.
- Reproduction: point a scratch PATH entry named `rtk` at a missing symlink
  target, run capability re-check once with `--format json` and once with
  `--format text`, and compare the divergence. The exact observed text is in
  the evidence transcript.
- Likely repair boundary: render executable `probe` / `evidence` details in the
  text divergence path, then add public text characterization for candidate
  absence, `broken-link`, `link-cycle`, and `not-executable`.
