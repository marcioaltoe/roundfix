Candido, Melo, dAmorim, Test Suite Parallelization in Open-Source Projects, ASE 2017: https://damorim.github.io/publications/candido-etal-ase17.pdf, page 8, lines 878-886. Reported failures: 97.5% null dereferences, 1.6% unsynchronized access and 0.8% likely broken dependencies. PRD attribution is supported. These Java-project results motivate race/order checks; they do not establish Go-suite rates or prove this conversion.
Mondal et al., Soundy Automated Parallelization of Test Execution, ICSME 2021: https://damorim.github.io/publications/MondalETAL_ICSME21.pdf, pages 1 and 3, abstract and lines 238-247. The study identifies data races and broken test dependencies as independent hazards. This supports running both race and shuffle checks, without endorsing rerunning failures to obtain green.
