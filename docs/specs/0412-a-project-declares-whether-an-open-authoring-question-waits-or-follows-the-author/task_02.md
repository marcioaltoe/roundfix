# Task 02: A Delegated Decision is read from the PRD, checked and listed in the status view

## Overview

A **Delegated Decision** gets its fixed form in the PRD's `## Decisions` section: a head line `- delegated: YYYY-MM-DD, <phase>` and five field lines. One parser in `internal/speccheck` reads the entries; the Clarification Record's check reports, under `SC-DECISIONS-UNRECORDED`, an entry it cannot read, and still never counts an entry as a person's answer; and the status view of `roundfix follow-up --status` lists the entries of every active Spec under `Delegated decisions: <n>` and in the JSON field `delegated_decisions`. The Clarification Record guide and the PRD template of `write-prd` give the form, and the `spec` and `follow-up` guides and references describe the finding and the list.
