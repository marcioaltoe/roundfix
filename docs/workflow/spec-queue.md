The one dependency-and-risk-ordered queue of approved Specs that
`docs/agents/autonomous-work.md` requires the Supervisor to maintain. The
Supervisor works this list top to bottom. A Spec leaves the list when it
archives.

## Why this order

Reordered on 2026-08-03 against a full session of evidence rather than
estimates. Three Specs closed that day (0071, 0072, 0074), and the cost was
not implementation: six Tasks of real work took 84 minutes, while closing one
Spec took four gate executions and two resolve Runs.

Where that time went, in order:

1. **Artifact contradictions** (0064). F-001 blocked Spec 0072's close four
   times for a governance defect that reading two files would have caught
   before any gate ran.
2. **One finding suppressing every row** (0070). The same F-001 marked fifteen
   of twenty-four rows blocked, including every journey the Spec existed to
   prove, so four cycles reported the same fifteen.
3. **Run residue** (0068, 0066). Branches and worktrees reclaimed by hand
   repeatedly; two commands refused until they were.

Those five Specs come first because each one makes the five after it cheaper.
The two user-facing defects (0059, 0073) follow — both real, both with
workarounds, and neither blocking the others. 0065 is last because Spec 0071
already delivered its verification-honesty half.

0063 archived on 2026-08-03 without implementation: Spec 0071's measurements
killed its cold-cache premise, and its two live features moved into 0070.
0056, 0057, 0071, 0072, and 0074 archived and left this list.
