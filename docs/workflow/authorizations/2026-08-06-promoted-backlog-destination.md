# Tooling authorization — a promoted backlog entry's destination

**Granted:** 2026-08-06 by the maintainer, in response to the seam recorded in
`docs/findings/2026-08-06-a-promoted-backlog-entry-has-nowhere-valid-to-go.md`:
*"Você está autorizado a mudar o archive-spec e demais arquivos necessários."*

**Scope:** teach the adoption index the type the typed backlog introduced, move
the entries already promoted, and give the rule a detector.

## Bounded files

- `.agents/skills/archive-spec/**` and its `skills/archive-spec/**` mirror,
  regenerated by `make skills-sync`.
- `.agents/skills/write-prd/**` and its `skills/write-prd/**` mirror: the same
  index contract is documented in its adoption steps, and leaving the two
  disagreeing would put the defect back one skill over.
- `internal/speccheck/**` for the detector, its carrier fixture, and the corpus
  characterization it moves.
- `docs/backlog/**` and `docs/specs/0080-cheap-detectors-run-before-the-gate/references/**`
  and `docs/specs/0081-a-journal-cheap-to-write-and-keep/references/**` for the
  three entries already promoted and their indexes.

Deterministic digest fallout of the skill edits is sanctioned by ADR-0081. A
hand-edited pin remains unauthorized.

## Outside this grant

- The Backlog Operational Contract's wording in
  `internal/baseline/assets/modules/context-workflow.json`. The clause already
  says what to do; this work makes it possible and checkable, and does not
  reword it.
- Any change to what a backlog type means or to the type set itself, which
  ADR-0092 owns.
