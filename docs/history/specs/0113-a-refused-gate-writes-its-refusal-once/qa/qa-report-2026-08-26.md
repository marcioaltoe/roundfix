**Impact: Blocks-Completion.** The Spec's stated goal — a gate that refuses at a
precondition leaves behind an artifact its own contract accepts — is not
delivered on any path a Supervisor reaches.

**Expected.** When the gate stops at a precondition check, the report it leaves
carries one terminal row `| 0 | blocked | precondition |`, frontmatter
`rows_blocked_precondition: 1`, and the refusing check and its reason
(PRD Core Features 1 and 3; TechSpec Acceptance 1 and 4).

**Actual.** `spec.WritePreconditionRefusalReport` is correct and complete, and
`speccheck.PreconditionRefusal` derives exactly what it needs — but nothing
calls either one. The only non-test reference to the writer in the whole tree is
its own definition. The Daemon's `qaMechanicalRequest`
(`internal/daemon/task_engine.go:2066-2076`) leaves `MechanicalRequest.
Precondition` at its zero value, so `result.PreconditionRefused` is always
false in production; `writeMechanicalQAReport`
(`internal/daemon/task_engine.go:2194-2205`) emits `verdict` plus the three
pre-existing counts and never `rows_blocked_precondition`,
`precondition_check`, or `precondition_reason`. The gate the PRD actually
describes — the one that runs `roundfix spec check <slug> --strict` and stops
before building its matrix — is the qa-gate skill, and no skill file was changed
by this Spec, so nothing tells a refusing gate what to write.
